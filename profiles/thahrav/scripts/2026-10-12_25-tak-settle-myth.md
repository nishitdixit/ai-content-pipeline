# 25 tak settle myth

**Idea:** 25 tak settle: who set this deadline?

> **Before recording**
> - none

## Hook (0-3s): pick one
A: 25 tak settle ho jaana zaroori hai?
B: Tum bhi sochte ho ki deadline 25 ki hai?

## Relate (3-15s)
Har friend ke Insta pe shaadi, job, gaadi... main bhi sochta tha main peeche ho raha hoon. Raat ko LinkedIn scroll karte hue lagta tha sab aage nikal gaye.

## Reframe (15-45s)
Ruko zara. Ye 25 ki deadline kahaan se aayi? Na tumhare parents ne, na tumhara dil. Ye society ka ek narrative hai—"abhi settle ho jao". Kabhi koi 22 mein startup chalata hai, kabhi 38 mein apna sukoon milta hai. Dono sahi. Zindagi koi train nahi jo ek fixed station pe rukti ho. Ye ek safar hai, aur har mod pe tum apna raasta chunte ho. Apni khushi ko kisi dusre ki timetable se mat naap.

## Takeaway (45-55s)
Aaj ek minute lo, apni khud ki timeline likho—sirf tumhare liye, koi aur nahi.

## Soft close (55-60s)
Tumhara samay abhi aata hai. Aaram se.
