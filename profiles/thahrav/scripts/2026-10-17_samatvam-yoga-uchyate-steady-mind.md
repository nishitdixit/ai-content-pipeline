# Samatvam yoga uchyate - steady mind

**Sat 17 Oct 2026** · P1 Purani Wisdom · Gita ki Ek Line
**Idea:** "Samatvam yoga uchyate": staying steady in success and failure *is* yoga (source to verify: Gita 2.48)

> **Before recording**
> - Verify 1 quote(s) before recording

## Hook (0-3s): pick one
A: Success ya failure, tum kabhi thehro?
B: Koi bhi halat, yoga ka secret?

## Relate (3-15s)
Kabhi promotion milti hai, toh excitement se phone ki ghanti bajti hai. Phir project fail ho jata, aur dimag kaam nahi karta. Main bhi aisa feel karta tha.

## Reframe (15-45s)
Bhagavad Gita, adhyay 2, shlok 48 mein Krishna kehte hain, “Samatvam yoga uchyate.” Matlab, sukh‑dukh, jeet‑haar mein ek samaan man banaye rakhna hi yog hai. Aaj ke fast‑pace job mein, har notification ek naya roller‑coaster lagta hai. Par jab hum apne reactions ko observe karte hain—khushi mein over‑react na karna, gile‑shikwe mein bhi shaant rehna—hum usi yoga ko daily practice kar rahe hote hain. Yeh koi alag meditation nahi, bas ek simple mindset shift hai: jo bhi aata hai, usko ek neutral screen pe rakhna.

## Takeaway (45-55s)
Aaj jab bhi koi achhi ya badi khabar aaye, ek gehri saans le, uss moment ko neutral label de, aur dekho kaise tum steady rehte ho.

## Soft close (55-60s)
Ruko zara, sab theek rahega.
