# Busy nahi, productive

**Wed 14 Oct 2026** · P3 Gentle Productivity
**Idea:** Busy hona productive hona nahi hai

> **Before recording**
> - none

## Hook (0-3s): pick one
A: Busy rehna, productive rehna ek hi cheez nahi
B: Kya tum bhi har roz sirf kaam se bhare ho?

## Relate (3-15s)
Raat ko phone ka notification, subah ka inbox, aur har din ek naya "must do" list. Main bhi aisa feel karta tha—jab kuch kaam nahi karta, guilt aati hai ki aaram karna galat hai.

## Reframe (15-45s)
Lekin socho zara—productivity ka matlab hai kam kaam, behtar kaam. Jab hum har cheez karne ki koshish karte hain, dhyan bikhar jaata hai, quality girti hai. Ek hi task par poora focus dena, aur beech‑beech mein guilt‑free break lena, actually output ko double kar deta hai. "Kama kaam, dhyan kaam"—jitna kam tum sach mein karte ho, utna hi tum zyada achieve karte ho. Yeh ek habit hai, race nahi.

## Takeaway (45-55s)
Aaj ek hi kaam choose karo, usko 45 minute tak poori attention ke saath karo, phir 15 minute aaram ka break lo—bina guilt ke.

## Soft close (55-60s)
Thoda thehro, sukoon se kaam karo.
