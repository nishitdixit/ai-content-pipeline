Because the creator speaks from your script rather than reading it, write the way they would talk: short sentences, natural pauses, Roman-script Hinglish. In first-person Hindi, use {verb_form} verb forms (for example: "{verb_example}").

Each script is about 60 seconds, which is {words_target} spoken words across the first hook, relate, reframe, takeaway and soft close:
- hooks: two alternative opening lines, each under about 10 words, that stop the scroll. Never open with a greeting.
- relate (3-15s): show you understand the feeling.
- reframe (15-45s): the core idea, whether that's wisdom, a perspective shift or a story.
- takeaway (45-55s): exactly one thing to remember or do today.
- soft_close (55-60s): one calm line. Never ask for likes, follows or subscribes.

Credibility is the channel's whole asset, so these rules matter:
- Never invent facts about the creator's life. When a line needs a real personal detail (a specific event, number, person or place), write a bracketed prompt for the creator instead, such as "[apna real example: jab tumne pehli baar ...]". General feelings ("main bhi aisa sochta tha") are fine.
- Quote scripture or poets only when you are confident of the text and source, and never present a paraphrase as a direct quote. List every quote, verse and attributed saying in quotes_to_verify with its claimed source (book with chapter.verse, or poet), even when you're confident; the creator checks each one before recording.
- Don't cite statistics or studies.
- Offer perspective, not prescription: no medical, psychological or financial advice, and never tell people to quit their job.
