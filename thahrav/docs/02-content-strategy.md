# 02: Content Strategy

## Core format
| Attribute | Decision |
|---|---|
| Format | Vertical (9:16) talking-head video, my face on camera |
| Length | 45-75 seconds, with 60s as the sweet spot |
| Language | Natural Hinglish (mostly Hinglish, Hindi or English where it comes naturally) |
| Frequency | 1 per day, 7 days a week |
| Platforms | YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (the same video on both) |
| Subtitles | Always burned in, in Roman Hinglish (most people watch muted) |
| Structure | Hook → Relate → Reframe → One takeaway → Soft close |

## Content pillars
Every video belongs to exactly one pillar. The weekly mix keeps the channel varied but still recognisable.

| # | Pillar | What it covers | Share of videos |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Purani Wisdom** | One idea from the Gita, Upanishads, Kabir, Chanakya or folk wisdom, applied to a modern problem | ~30% (2/week) |
| P2 | **Rush Reframes** | Taking apart the myths that make people rush: timelines, comparison, FOMO, "settle ho jao" | ~30% (2/week) |
| P3 | **Gentle Productivity** | Doing fewer things better, rest without guilt, focus, saying no | ~15% (1/week) |
| P4 | **Chhoti Practices** | One tiny practice to try today: breathing, a phone-free morning, a walk, a gratitude line | ~15% (1/week) |
| P5 | **Meri Kahani** | My own experience as an engineer who used to rush, and what I'm learning | ~10% (1/week) |

### Sample week

## Recurring series
Named series build recognition and make finding ideas easier.

| Series | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **"Ruko Zara"** | P2 | Start with a common rushing behaviour, then pause and reframe it |
| **"Gita ki Ek Line"** | P1 | One verse with the chapter.verse cited, a plain meaning, and a modern application |
| **"Aaj ka Ek Kaam"** | P4 | One small action for today |
| **"Comment ka Jawab"** | Any | Answer a real viewer comment (from week 3 onward) |

## Video structure
Every script follows this. See [the script template](../content/script-template.md).

| Section | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | 0-3s | Stop the scroll. A question, a bold line, or a feeling the viewer recognises. |
| **Relate** | 3-15s | "Main bhi..." / "Tumhe bhi lagta hai na..." Show I understand. |
| **Reframe** | 15-45s | The core idea: wisdom, a perspective shift, or a story. |
| **Takeaway** | 45-55s | One clear thing to remember or do. Only one. |
| **Soft close** | 55-60s | A calm line, e.g. "Aaram se. Zindagi race nahi hai." No begging for likes. |

## Platforms
**Phase 1-3: YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels only.** They're the two biggest short-video platforms in India, and the same vertical video works on both.

| Platform | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | Discovery and long-term searchability | Titles and descriptions matter for search |
| Instagram Reels | Discovery and community (DMs, Stories, shares) | Shares and saves matter most |
| Later: YouTube long-form | Depth, e.g. a weekly 8-12 min video | Only after day 90, if there's demand |
| Later: WhatsApp Channel | Daily line or affirmation, a bridge to the app | After 1k followers |

## Posting rhythm
- **One video every day.** A missed day is fine, but two in a row isn't.
- **Posting time:** start at **7:30 PM IST**, after the commute and before dinner. Test a morning slot around 8 AM in month 2. The data decides.
- **Buffer:** always keep **3 recorded videos in reserve** so a sick day or a busy day doesn't break the streak.

## Title, caption and hashtag rules
- **Titles** (YouTube): under 60 characters, in Hinglish, written as a question or a direct statement. "25 tak settle hona zaroori hai?"
- **Captions** (Instagram): 2-4 lines that extend the idea, plus one question to invite comments.
- **Hashtags:** 3-5, mixing broad and niche tags (#hinglish #slowliving #bhagavadgita #mentalpeace #lifelessons). Don't stuff in 30.
- **Thumbnails** (Shorts cover): a frame with my face and 2-4 words of text.

## What we never make
- Fear-based or doomsday hooks ("Tum barbaad ho jaoge agar...")
- Hustle bait ("5 AM club join karo")
- Content that shames people who are ambitious
- Medical, financial or mental-health *advice*. Perspective is fine, prescriptions aren't.
- Scripture we haven't verified, or misattributed quotes
- Copying another creator's script or structure line for line
