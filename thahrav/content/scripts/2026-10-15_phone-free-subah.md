# Phone-free Subah

**Thu 15 Oct 2026** · P4 Chhoti Practices · Aaj ka Ek Kaam
**Idea:** Subah ke pehle 20 minute: no phone

> **Before recording**
> - none

## Hook (0-3s): pick one
A: Subah phone se door rehna mushkil lagta hai?
B: 20 minute phone-free, life change karega?

## Relate (3-15s)
Mujhe bhi subah alarm bajte hi phone pakadna instinct lagta tha. Whatsapp, news, LinkedIn—sab pehle aane ki race. Har notification se heart thoda tez dhadakne lagta, aur din shuru hi stress se ho jata.

## Reframe (15-45s)
Par socho zara, jab phone haath mein nahi hota, dimag ko ek break milta hai. Pehli 20 minute bas saans le, paani pi, ya khidki ke bahar dekho. Yeh chhoti si pause, din bhar ki clarity aur stress kam karne ka seedha raasta hai. Tumhare thoughts ko organize karta hai, bina notifications ke. Is pause se tumhara brain default mode network reset hota hai, jo creativity ko boost karta hai. Aur jab tum phone ko ignore karte ho, tum apne aap se connect karte ho—apni thoughts, goals, gratitude.

## Takeaway (45-55s)
Aaj subah alarm ke baad pehle 20 minute phone band rakho. Sirf ek glass paani, 5 gehri saans, ya thoda walk. Phir din shuru karo.

## Soft close (55-60s)
Thoda thehro, din ko sukoon se shuru karo.
