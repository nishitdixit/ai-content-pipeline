# LinkedIn highlight reel vs real movie

**Fri 16 Oct 2026** · P2 Rush Reframes · Ruko Zara
**Idea:** LinkedIn pe sab successful kyun lagte hain? A highlight reel vs your whole movie

> **Before recording**
> - none

## Hook (0-3s): pick one
A: LinkedIn pe sab success story lagte hain?
B: Tum bhi feel karte ho sab aage?

## Relate (3-15s)
Main bhi roz LinkedIn scroll karta hoon, aur sochta hoon ki sab log promotion, award, ya naya startup launch kar rahe hain. Kabhi kabhi dil kehta hai—main kahin peeche reh gaya.

## Reframe (15-45s)
Par socho zara—LinkedIn ek highlight reel hai. Jaise movie ka trailer, sirf best scenes dikhata hai, beech ki struggles, late nights, ya failed prototypes chhupa deta hai. Tumhara real movie har din ka ek chhota frame hai. Ek din koi milestone milta hai, agle din setback. Dono hi tumhe aage le jaate hain. Jab tum sirf trailer dekhte ho, tum apni speed ko dusro ke fast forward se compare karte ho, jo unfair hai. Apni story ko full length mein dekhna start karo.

## Takeaway (45-55s)
Aaj ek ghanta LinkedIn se door raho, aur ek page apni real progress ka likho—chahe woh chhoti coding challenge ho ya ek nayi skill.

## Soft close (55-60s)
Thoda thehro, tumhara safar tumhara hi hai.
Agar ye feeling bahut heavy hai, kisi se baat karo — dost, family, ya professional. Tele-MANAS helpline 14416 pe free baat kar sakte ho.
