# Script Template

Every script, whether written by hand or by the engine, follows this format. Target length: **130-160 spoken words, which is about 60 seconds.**

---

```
DATE:       YYYY-MM-DD
PILLAR:     P1 Purani Wisdom | P2 Rush Reframes | P3 Gentle Productivity | P4 Chhoti Practices | P5 Meri Kahani
SERIES:     Ruko Zara | Gita ki Ek Line | Aaj ka Ek Kaam | Comment ka Jawab | —
WORKING TITLE:

── HOOK (0-3s) — pick one ──
A:
B:

── RELATE (3-15s) ──
(Show I understand: "Main bhi...", "Tumhe bhi lagta hai na...")

── REFRAME (15-45s) ──
(The core idea: wisdom / perspective / story)

── TAKEAWAY (45-55s) ──
(ONE line to remember or do)

── SOFT CLOSE (55-60s) ──
(Calm line, e.g. "Aaram se. Zindagi race nahi hai.")

── ON-SCREEN HOOK TEXT ──  (≤ 6 words)
── COVER TEXT ──           (2-4 words)

── YOUTUBE TITLE ── (< 60 chars, two options)
1.
2.

── INSTAGRAM CAPTION ── (2-4 lines + one question)

── HASHTAGS ── (3-5)

── QUOTES TO VERIFY ──
- "<quote>" — claimed source: <book, chapter.verse> — ☐ verified

── SENSITIVE TOPIC? ── yes/no → if yes, add the helpline line to the close
```

---

## Worked example

```
DATE:       2026-10-12
PILLAR:     P2 Rush Reframes
SERIES:     Ruko Zara
WORKING TITLE: 25 tak settle

── HOOK ──
A: "25 tak settle ho jaana chahiye — ye deadline di kisne?"
B: "Tumhe bhi lagta hai tum late ho gaye?"

── RELATE ──
Shaadi, ghar, gaadi, achhi job — sab 25-26 tak. Ye list humne
kahin se utha li hai. Main bhi har birthday pe ye list check
karta tha... aur har baar lagta tha main fail ho raha hoon.

── REFRAME ──
Par socho zara — ye timeline kisne banayi? Na tumne, na
tumhare dil ne. Kisi ne 22 mein startup banaya, kisi ne 40
mein apna pehla sukoon dhoondha. Dono sahi hain. Zindagi koi
train nahi hai jo ek fixed time pe station pe pahunchni hai.
Ye ek safar hai — aur safar ka maza tab hai jab tum khidki
ke bahar dekh bhi sako.

── TAKEAWAY ──
Aaj apni list ka ek item uthao aur poocho — "Ye mujhe chahiye,
ya mujhe bataya gaya hai ki chahiye?"

── SOFT CLOSE ──
Tum late nahi ho. Aaram se.

── ON-SCREEN HOOK TEXT ── 25 tak settle? Kisne kaha?
── COVER TEXT ── Tum late nahi ho

── YOUTUBE TITLE ──
1. 25 tak settle hona zaroori hai?
2. Tum late nahi ho — ye suno

── INSTAGRAM CAPTION ──
Ye timeline humne kab accept kar li?
Kisi ki zindagi 22 mein shuru hoti hai, kisi ki 40 mein.
Tumhari list mein aisa kya hai jo sach mein tumhara hai? 👇

── HASHTAGS ── #hinglish #slowliving #lifelessons #comparison #mentalpeace

── QUOTES TO VERIFY ── none

── SENSITIVE TOPIC? ── no
```
